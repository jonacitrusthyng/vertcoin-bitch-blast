The final sung phrasing has not been exhaustively compared with every draft lyric.

Track 22, **No Permission Slip**, is Jona's previously approved original MP3 master. SHA-256: `cad01a2ea9647f3450dd4028bdfbe8c236433525c9644615fe5fe3dd011e4164`.

All 22 music files are bundled locally. Their hashes and durations appear in Audio-Verification.json. No streaming or external accounts are needed to play them.

## Sound effects and artwork

The five short effects (click, coin, stop, save, blast) were synthesized locally from oscillator/noise envelopes for this release. No third-party effect recordings were sampled. The artwork is Jona's provided/generated material; the patch background edit is described in ARTWORK.md. Emi's fan interpretation is inspired by Katawa Shoujo / Four Leaf Studios.

One alternate Skyward Signal generation was submitted before the finished fan-site album was discovered. It is **not** included in this release; all 21 album tracks use the existing recordings.
